# Step 4A — Sign selection

**Prepared:** 1 October 2026. **Status:** 4A complete — selection and explicit research gaps only.

## Basis and scope

Reviewed the [editable manuscript](manuscript.md), its Appendix B, the [approved outline](curriculum-outline.md) and the [existing sign inventory](road-sign-inventory.json). Codes below are selection identifiers from the saved catalogue inventory, not a fresh certification of current meanings or image variants. No source-book names, page numbers or ordering are used for the new reference.

The selected set supports the manuscript’s concrete examples and representative coverage of every Appendix B family. It is not an exhaustive official sign catalogue or a claim that omitted signs cannot appear in the theory test. All selected codes belong in the illustrated reference, grouped by the decisions below; lesson use is indicated separately. Repeated codes reuse the same verified asset where appropriate. A code can need several graphics.

Step 4B will verify official detail pages, availability and reuse. Step 4C will collect and inspect files; 4D will verify final Swedish/English labels and explanations; 4E will choose precise passage placements. The descriptions below are editorial selection purposes, not publication-ready captions. No downloads, visual design or app changes are part of 4A.

## Coverage review

- Explicit manuscript codes B1/B2, M1/M3/M8/M9, T6–T8 and T16–T19 are selected.
- Narrowing, children, fuel, advance directions, lane controls, panel distances, parking times, pedestrian/cycle crossings, special streets, motorways, railways, roadworks, dimensional restrictions, environmental zones and tunnel exits have selection groups above.
- Appendix B families A/B/C/D/E/F/G/H/I/J/S/T/M/SIG/P/V/X/Y all have selected representatives. Police and guard directions and railway signals are retained even where older local indexes were incomplete.
- Generic mentions of a driver signalling, spotting a defect or noticing a warning are not automatically requests for road-sign artwork. Equipment and contextual-image needs are explicitly separated above.
- Lessons without a sign-specific teaching need use no added sign merely for decoration. First-aid guidance needs no road-sign illustration.
