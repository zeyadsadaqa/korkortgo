# Swedish illustration style reference — 5E

**Prepared:** 3 October 2026. **Status:** Preview awaiting approval; not a production asset sheet.

[Style board v1](style-v1.png) sets a shared visual vocabulary: teal learner estate, ochre other-traffic car, white delivery van, varied pedestrians and cyclist, neutral police appearance study, and urban/rural Swedish settings. Use the [brand guide](../../../../design/brand/README.md) and [illustration instructions](../../../../design/brand/illustration-guide.md) alongside it.

## Consistency rules

Keep the same car body, roof, lamps and colour across each sequence. Use right-hand traffic, scene-appropriate white road markings and Swedish road furniture. People should retain clothing/appearance within a scenario; varied ages and backgrounds are appropriate. Scene geometry and learning clarity take priority over scenery. The hand-painted editorial style is for contextual illustrations; official signs retain their original artwork.

The user-provided [first](../../../../design/mockups/01-learn-and-prepare.png) and [second](../../../../design/mockups/02-practise-and-review.png) boards are style references, not factual evidence. The generated style board was used as an internal continuity reference for the scenario previews in this package; none of these new images has yet received user approval.

## Police appearance evidence

Consulted the official [PMFS 2026:18 uniform document](https://polisen.se/2f27ccebd5a45e4c44e774d23515d446/siteassets/forfattningssamling/fap-nummer/fap798-01-pmfs2026_18.pdf) on 3 October 2026: chapter 4 §4 supports the dark-blue baseline, and the garment table on printed page 34 describes the reflective yellow m/12 vest with blue-backed function identification. The official [register](https://polisen.se/lagar-och-regler/polismyndighetens-forfattningssamling/1/?lpfm.srt=du) identifies this newer document. Earlier 2022/2024 and 2026:4 versions found during research were not treated as the current reference.

The officer here stands neutrally; no traffic direction is taught. Detailed badges, rank, cut and equipment remain simplified and must not be treated as an exact uniform plate. Recheck these details before producing a close-up or traffic-control scene. Use verified P-series artwork and the relevant official meaning for instructional gestures. No official photograph or PDF illustration was copied into the generated board; consulting a source is not a licence to reproduce its photographs.

## Visual review and refinements

The urban and rural samples show the car on the right side and use white centre markings. The vehicle sheet is generally coherent, but generated grille emblems should be removed for final generic vehicle art, and the estate proportions need to be locked across angles. Officer insignia are approximate and need reference-based refinement. Do not propagate either limitation as an approved detail merely because the style is accepted.

The board's claim “Same unbranded car as in the app” is generated presentation copy, not an asset-identity guarantee. The car family is a new proposal inspired by the reference style. Its final approved files must be tracked individually with captions, alternative text and dimensions.
