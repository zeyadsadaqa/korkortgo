Required corrections and limits:

- D07 and the assessment parking panel still need a visible access lane beyond the rear footway, with a plausible vehicle departure route. Do not approve their geometry for instruction as drawn.
- D06 tick spacing is approximate. Correct it in production or label it not to scale. Keep the 12:00 worked result separate from any unanswered assessment version.
- Final generic vehicles should omit generated grille emblems; police insignia need detail review before close-up use.
- Sign-layout typography drifted toward serif body text. Production must follow the guide's Source Sans 3 body type, checked 4D captions and exact official sign files.
- F1-2/Y2 boxes are flow examples, not actual print-size approval. In particular Y2's size limit is only 10.2 × 5.1 mm at 300 ppi.
- Source references, selectable text, alt text, navigation and accessibility need the real PDF implementation and inspection; the raster boards do not provide those features.
