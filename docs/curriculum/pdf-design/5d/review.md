## Teaching hierarchy and reading order

Use a stable lesson ID, editorial title and goal before the explanation. Requirements retain an explicit written label and nearby official source reference. Learning approaches and worked decisions remain explicitly labelled editorial teaching material. For official recommendations in other lessons, use the written label “Official recommendation” and name the authority beside the passage; use a mist-tinted panel with navy text when emphasis is needed. Do not style advice as an unlabelled legal requirement. These sample pages demonstrate requirements versus learning approaches; the recommendation-panel treatment is specified here rather than fabricated from an unrelated lesson.

Keep calculations separate from rules and preserve their assumptions. M03-L04 visibly identifies its conversion as an illustrative calculation, not a prediction of stopping distance. Show a knowledge-check question before a separately labelled answer, with enough separation to encourage an attempt. Keep the answer in the same reading sequence; do not imply it is hidden in a static PDF. Use a short reminder to finish a lesson. Continue long lessons across as many pages as necessary instead of shrinking body text.

Reserve an illustration area adjacent to the relevant explanation without inventing artwork. The D01 box on the preview is a production placeholder only; 5E must review the actual contextual diagram. The placeholder text must not appear in the finished book.

The practice record includes all nine manuscript prompts, blank for the learner to complete. Allow additional space or continuation pages for longer responses in production.
