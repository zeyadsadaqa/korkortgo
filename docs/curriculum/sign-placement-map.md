## Production rules

- Resolve caption_id in the bound sign-captions.json; retain its English editorial-translation status, exact variant meaning and source reference.
- Use only the bound preferred_asset when eligibility is true. Retain its 300 ppi maximum dimensions, aspect ratio and all meaningful artwork. Final legibility at actual size is checked in step 6.
- Present comparison sets as separately labelled examples. They are not verified sign assemblies, road scenes or a continuous real-world route.
- Use alt text with the teaching caption; preserve reading order and text equivalents for motion, flashing and sound. Do not rely on colour or image alone.
- Place the Transportstyrelsen artwork credit and official source links/dates in the final references; preserve the learning-aid disclaimer prominently.
- Reference groups are proposed subsections of Appendix B. Repeated selected codes have one primary entry plus cross-references. Family tags supply a separate lookup without duplicating artwork.
- No unapproved contextual diagram, held image or fabricated official artwork may be rendered from this map.

**Artwork credit:** Road-sign illustrations: Transportstyrelsen. Source links and verification dates are recorded in the official references.

### LP001 · M03-L01

**Location:** After the paragraph at manuscript line 213, in “M03-L01 · Recognise what a sign asks”.

> Start with purpose, then read the specific symbol and any supplementary panel. The official catalogue groups signs by function. Do not rely on colour alone: similar shapes can communicate different duties, and some important signs have distinctive shapes. [Transportstyrelsen: sign catalogue][signs]

**Codes:** A15, B1, C31, D1, E5

**Together/context:** Separate examples, not a roadside assembly; read the full caption rather than infer a rule from colour.

**Mode:** labelled_recognition_comparison.

### LP002 · M03-L01

**Location:** After the paragraph at manuscript line 225, in “M03-L01 · Recognise what a sign asks”.

> **Requirements.** B1 means yield to vehicles on the crossing road or route. Make that intention clear early and proceed only without danger or obstruction. B2 requires a stop before entering the crossing road or track area. Stop at the stop line; without one, stop immediately before the signal or before entering the crossing road or track area, as applicable. A rolling pause does not meet a stop duty. [Road Signs Regulation, B1–B2][sign-law] · [Traffic Regulation, chapter 3 §§5 and 19][traffic-law]

**Codes:** B1, B2, M13, M14, M18

### LP004 · M03-L02

**Location:** After the paragraph at manuscript line 237, in “M03-L02 · Combine the instructions”.

> **Requirements.** Follow the traffic instructions that apply to you. Where an instruction differs from a general traffic rule, the instruction governs. A steady traffic-light indication takes precedence over a yield or stop sign; a police officer's direction takes precedence over the rules and ordinary traffic instructions. This is not a rule that a green signal cancels every other restriction. Authorised guards also give traffic directions that must be followed. [Traffic Regulation, chapter 2 §§2–4][traffic-law]

**Codes:** P1, P2, V1, V3

**Learning purpose:** Introduce the precedence of authorised directions and recognise the guard identifier.

**Together/context:** Use separate labelled illustrations with the existing hierarchy paragraph. Motion is described in words, not simulated by a static montage.

**Mode:** labelled_recognition_comparison.

### LP005 · M03-L02

**Location:** After the paragraph at manuscript line 239, in “M03-L02 · Combine the instructions”.

> For ordinary vehicle signals: red means stop; red and amber together still mean stop, with green approaching. Green permits movement, subject to other duties and space ahead. Amber means stop unless, when green changes to amber, you are already so close that stopping would be dangerous. Flashing amber requires particular caution; read the signs and applicable rules. Arrow signals apply to the direction indicated. [Road Signs Regulation, chapter 3 §§3–6][sign-law]

**Codes:** SIG1, SIG2, SIG3, SIG4, SIG5

**Learning purpose:** Read ordinary vehicle indications and the distinction between amber and flashing amber.

**Together/context:** Present red, red/amber, green and amber in that order; flashing amber is a separate condition, not the next stage in the normal cycle. Retain arrow scope and the late-change exception.

**Mode:** labelled_recognition_comparison.
