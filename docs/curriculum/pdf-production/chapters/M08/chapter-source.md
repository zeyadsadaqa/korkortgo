## 8. Take responsibility for the vehicle and load

Permission to drive, technical capacity and actual loading are separate checks. Passing one does not mean the others are satisfied.

### M08-L01 · Understand what you may drive

**Your goal:** Use entitlement and vehicle information to assess a car or trailer combination.

**Requirements.** Ordinary category B permits a passenger car or light goods vehicle with a total weight of no more than 3,500 kg. For trailers, either the trailer's total weight is at most 750 kg, or, if above 750 kg, the car and trailer's combined total weights must not exceed 3,500 kg. The first route can therefore permit a combination up to 4,250 kg; it does not mean every 4,250 kg combination is permitted on ordinary B. [Category B][b] · [Car with trailer][trailer]

Extended B (B96) permits combined total weights up to 4,250 kg, without increasing the car's 3,500 kg limit. Under the ordinary current BE rule, the towing car is still limited to 3,500 kg total weight and the trailer or trailers may have combined total weights up to 3,500 kg. Older entitlements and conditions can differ; check the individual licence rather than applying a current-new-licence example to everyone. This book is not a full B96 or BE course. [Trailer entitlement guide][trailer-guide]

**Two tests.** Entitlement uses registered total weights. Technical towing limits also restrict what the particular car may pull in its actual loaded state. Emptying a trailer may reduce its actual weight without changing its registered total weight, so it may solve a technical-weight problem but not an entitlement problem. [Towing weights][towing]

**Worked decision.** A car has a total weight of 2,200 kg and a trailer 1,500 kg. Their combined total weight is 3,700 kg, and the trailer exceeds 750 kg. Ordinary B does not cover this combination. B96 may satisfy the entitlement part, but the car's towing capacity and loaded weights still need checking.

**Knowledge check.** A 3,500 kg car is paired with a 750 kg trailer. Is ordinary B automatically excluded because the sum exceeds 3,500 kg? **Answer:** No. The trailer-at-most-750 kg route can cover it. Technical and actual-weight limits must still be satisfied.

**Remember:** Check the licence first, then the vehicle and load—not one instead of the other.

### M08-L02 · Make the load part of the safety check

**Your goal:** Distinguish weight terms and assess securing, capacity and trailer suitability.

| Term | Meaning for this lesson |
|---|---|
| *Tjänstevikt* | Vehicle in its defined ready-for-use state; a car's figure includes the driver, while a trailer's does not include cargo. |
| *Totalvikt* | Registered maximum weight, built from the defined unladen/ready weight and maximum load. |
| *Bruttovikt* | Actual weight transmitted to the road at that time. |
| *Maximilast* | Total weight minus the defined ready weight: the maximum passenger/cargo load. |

Use the official definitions when reading registration data. Do not count the driver twice in an exercise that starts from a car's defined *tjänstevikt*, and do not assume a paper figure measures the load you have actually added. [Weight definitions][weights]

**Requirements.** Keep within the car's permitted trailer weight and maximum actual combined weight as well as each vehicle's limits. Registration fields O.1/O.2 identify relevant braked/unbraked trailer limits; F.3 identifies the maximum combined actual weight. Missing or unclear data needs resolution before towing. [Towing weights][towing]

Secure cargo so it cannot endanger people, fall, drag, obstruct control or obscure required lights and signals. Put heavy items low and suitably supported, and follow the equipment's rated load and securing instructions. A roof box is not permission to exceed a roof or vehicle limit. [Traffic Regulation, chapter 3 §§78–81][traffic-law] · [Safe loading][load-advice]

Projecting cargo needs separate checks. Mark cargo extending ahead or more than 1 m behind, with the specified exception for clearly visible front projection below 1 m. Darkness or poor visibility requires the prescribed lamps and reflectors, white forward and red rearward. Marking does not authorise an otherwise prohibited width, length or load. [Traffic Regulation, chapter 3 §§82–83 and chapter 4 §§15–17][traffic-law]

On roads other than private roads, the ordinary width/load rule restricts a motor vehicle or its trailer if the load projects more than 20 cm beyond either side, or the overall width including load exceeds 260 cm. The general length ceiling for a non-bus vehicle or combination is 24.0 m including load; special regimes and exemptions exist. Do not treat those general ceilings as permission to exceed a lower vehicle, route or sign limit. Special oversized transport needs its own verification. [Traffic Regulation, chapter 4 §§15–17][traffic-law]

For a conventional light braked trailer with rigid drawbar and suitable overrun brake, the maximum is normally 80 km/h. For an unbraked trailer up to 750 kg, 80 km/h requires its total weight to be no more than half the car’s defined ready weight; when the trailer is empty, its ready weight is used for this comparison instead. The ordinary unbraked combination outside those conditions is limited to 40 km/h, subject to the other combination requirements; other configurations can be limited to 30. Use Transportstyrelsen's calculator and guidance for the specific combination, and obey any lower road limit. [Trailer guide: speed conditions][trailer-guide] · [Traffic Regulation, chapter 4 §20, version applicable on 1 October 2026][traffic-law]

**Worked decision.** A trailer's ready weight is 400 kg and cargo adds 500 kg, giving an illustrative actual weight of 900 kg. A car rated to tow only 800 kg cannot pull that load merely because the driver's entitlement covers the registered total weights.

**Knowledge check.** Does a strap around the load prove the vehicle is within its weight limit? **Answer:** No. Securing and weight are different checks. Also inspect the correct coupling, lighting, tyres and equipment for the actual trailer before departure.

**Remember:** Establish what the load weighs, where it is, how it is restrained and what limits apply.

### M08-L03 · Keep the car legally ready

**Your goal:** Build an ownership checklist for registration, insurance, inspection and vehicle status.

**Requirements.** The registered owner of an on-road car must have compulsory motor insurance. Not using the car, or having a defect or driving prohibition, does not by itself end that obligation; formal off-road status matters. Compulsory insurance covers specified traffic injuries and damage to others, not every loss to your own car. Optional cover varies by policy. [Compulsory motor insurance][insurance]

For an ordinary car or light goods vehicle up to 3,500 kg, the normal inspection sequence is: first by 36 months after the month of first use, second by 24 months after the first inspection month, then by 14 months after the preceding inspection month. Check the recorded date and any special category or exemption for your vehicle. An earlier inspection changes the next calculation; an old reminder is not proof of the current deadline. [Inspection intervals][inspection]

An on-road registration status, valid insurance and completed inspection answer different questions. Check the vehicle's status and any prohibition before use, and deal with tax obligations through the current official service. A car being physically driveable does not establish legal readiness. [On-road and off-road status][status]

**Ownership process.** Transportstyrelsen currently provides eligible private-person transfers through Mina sidor; other cases may require the latest registration certificate part 2. The service has eligibility conditions. Consult the current instructions instead of relying on an old app or a remembered screen sequence. Part 1 and the vehicle enquiry service provide technical information; protect ownership documents appropriately. [Ownership transfer][owner] · [Registration certificate][registration]

**Worked decision.** A buyer collects an on-road car but assumes the seller's insurance is enough for the trip home. The buyer checks the transfer and arranges the required cover from the applicable ownership date before driving. The sale does not transfer the seller's insurance as personal cover for the buyer.

**Knowledge check.** Does parking an on-road car in a garage automatically remove the compulsory-insurance requirement? **Answer:** No. Formal status and the insurance rules apply, not simply whether you intend to drive today.

**Remember:** Keep a record of dates and obligations, then verify them against the current vehicle record.

### M08-L04 · Know what assistance cannot do

**Your goal:** Explain why safety systems and powertrain differences do not remove your duties to observe and control.

Trafikverket describes modern protection as including crashworthiness and systems such as lane assistance and automatic emergency braking. These functions can help, but a good safety rating is not a promise that any collision can be avoided. Recent official crash testing also shows the limits of automatic braking in severe truck-trailer collision scenarios. [Crash safety][crash] · [Trafikverket: braking and truck-trailer tests][aeb]

ABS is anti-lock braking. It helps retain the ability to develop steering and braking forces together, within available friction. Stability control reduces the risk of skid crashes; it does not create extra road grip or excuse excessive speed. The driver still has to maintain control and an appropriate speed. [VTI friction report, section 4.3][friction] · [Trafikverket: tyre and stability advice][tyre-advice] · [Traffic Regulation, chapter 3 §14][traffic-law]

**Powertrain awareness.** A plug-in hybrid can operate on electricity and with a combustion engine. That difference matters when interpreting fuel use and emissions: do not assume that every journey is electrically powered. [Transportstyrelsen: plug-in hybrids][hybrid]

For the actual vehicle, establish how it becomes ready to move, how drive and reverse are selected, how it is secured, and what warnings or assistance limitations need attention. Do not transfer a universal gear-change, engine-speed, coasting or brake-check recipe between manual, automatic, hybrid and electric vehicles. Vehicle-specific procedures need the vehicle's own instructions and practical teaching. The curriculum's role is to help you identify those questions, not guess their answers.

**Worked decision.** A driver assumes lane assistance will handle a temporary roadworks layout. Instead, they must read the actual signs and lane path, stay in control and understand the system's limitations. An assistance symbol does not transfer responsibility for the route.

**Knowledge check.** Does an anti-lock system guarantee a short stop on every surface? **Answer:** No. It operates within the friction available, and stopping still depends on speed, surface, tyres and conditions.

**Remember:** Know what the system assists, what it cannot guarantee and what remains your task.

### M08-R · Review and self-assessment

**Scenario:** You borrow a car and trailer for a move. You know the registration numbers but not the actual load weight or towing limit.

Explain the checks required before agreeing to drive. **Review guide:** Establish entitlement, registered and actual weights, technical limits, vehicle status, insurance, inspection, coupling, tyres, lights and securing. Do not let one successful calculator result stand in for every physical check.

Identify one figure or document you do not yet know how to interpret. Record the risk of guessing and the official reference you will use. [Syllabus self-assessment, chapters 2–3 §3][syllabus]
