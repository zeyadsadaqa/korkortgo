# G3 evidence

Verified 12 October 2026: three essential claims for three questions.

## Safe stopping distance

Transport for NSW / NSW Government (Australia). Live guidance; publication date not displayed in inspected passage. [Source](https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driving-nsw/roads-safety-and-rules/safe-driving/safe-stopping-distance).

Locator: Working out your crash avoidance space, steps 1–3; video transcript general-rule explanation. Short excerpt: “count how long it takes you to pass the same object as the vehicle in front of you”.

Support: Select a fixed roadside point; count three seconds between lead vehicle and own vehicle passing it; increase margins for poor conditions.

Scope: Technique only. Three seconds is presented as a guideline, not a Swedish statutory interval or universal guarantee. Australian reaction-time assumptions, long-vehicle laws, penalty rules and left-side placement instructions are not imported.

Retrieval: Direct primary page retrieved and inspected

Fallback: Exact fixed-point/counting instruction not located in targeted TS/TRV or Swedish-agency searches. Trafikverket-hosted NESTA and tunnel reports mention a three-second guideline but do not teach the counting method. Official NSW guidance supplies the exact technique.

## Taxi Driver Manual

Department for Infrastructure / Driver & Vehicle Agency, Northern Ireland government. Version 2.1 March 2024; hosted file August 2025. [Source](https://www.northernireland.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2025-08/taxi-manual-august-2025.pdf).

Locator: Printed/PDF p.83: Tailgating (zero-based PDF index 82). Short excerpt: “gradually reduce your speed to increase the gap between you and any vehicle ahead”.

Support: When followed too closely, gradually enlarge the forward gap to allow gentler braking and reduce rear-impact risk.

Scope: Common defensive-driving advice only, not Northern Ireland taxi law. Risk reduction is not a guarantee; necessary emergency braking remains necessary. Its two-second target is not imported.

Retrieval: Direct primary PDF retrieved and inspected

Fallback: Exact increase-forward-gap/gentler-braking behavioural instruction not located in inspected TS/TRV material. This government manual provides the explicit causal advice.

## KRAV Grundvärden, Vägar och gators utformning (VGU)

Trafikverket. TRV publication 2024:148, version 1.0; recognised by current Trafikverket VGU overview as part of current framework. [Source](https://trafikverket.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1909331/FULLTEXT01.pdf).

Locator: Printed p.9(58): braking-distance equation and variable definitions. Short excerpt: “Bromssträckan erhålls enligt formeln”.

Support: S_b = (V1² − V2²) / [2g(f_b + i)]; setting final speed to zero and holding denominator constant establishes the speed-squared braking-distance relationship.

Scope: Original mathematical derivation from agency equation. Hold grip/braking deceleration and gradient constant; real design friction can depend on speed. No general promise of exact fourfold road-test distance or fourfold total stopping distance.

Retrieval: Complete indexed primary-PDF equation passage retrieved through search. Direct PDF opens timed out; no local source download or checksum claimed.

Fallback: Preferred agency supplies the mathematical basis; no other publisher added for this claim.

The VGU equation is used for a stated constant-deceleration derivation, not as a fixed stopping-distance prediction. Current publication identity confirmed by [Trafikverket VGU overview](https://bransch.trafikverket.se/for-dig-i-branschen/vag/Utformning-av-vagar-och-gator/vagar-och-gators-utformning-vgu/).
