# G5 evidence

Checked 13 October 2026. Five essential claims for three questions.

## Traffic Regulation (1998:1276)

Swedish Government; published by Sveriges riksdag. Consolidated live text; chapter 3 §§43–45 checked as applicable 13 October 2026. [Source](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/trafikforordning-19981276_sfs-1998-1276/).

Locator: Chapter 3 §45: maximum permitted road speed ≤50, approaching-driver duty and bus caution; §43: safe edge/lateral movement and facilitating observed lane entry. Short excerpt: “utan fara eller onödigt hinder; anpassa hastigheten så att inkörningen underlättas; 50 kilometer i timmen eller lägre”.

Support: Special bus-stop departure duty at road limits ≤50; no such special duty at 70, but safe-entry and speed-adaptation/cooperation duties remain.

Scope: Ordinary departure from roadside bus stop into the approaching driver’s lane, subject to other applicable controls/rules. Road maximum limit, not actual vehicle speed, determines §45. No unconditional priority or permission to force a gap. “Must give way” in the keyed answer is explained as waiting for a safe entry under §43, not an invented separate always-yield statute.

Fallback: Inspected TS/TRV results did not establish the complete current 50/70 comparison and reciprocal duties. The current statutory provisions give exact primary legal support.

## Fakta om ledarhundar

Myndigheten för delaktighet (MFD). Last updated 30 April 2026. [Source](https://www.mfd.se/ledarhundsverksamheten/fakta-om-ledarhundar/).

Locator: När du möter ett ledarhundsekipage, first and third bullets; Så här arbetar ledarhundar. Short excerpt: “Stör inte ledarhundar som arbetar genom att klappa eller prata med dem.”.

Support: Avoid petting/talking to distract working guide dogs; distraction can endanger handler; communicate with handler, who chooses route and remains in control.

Scope: Safety/interaction advice, not a newly asserted traffic offence. No claim that the dog alone decides a road is safe or every glance necessarily distracts it.

Fallback: No exact guide-dog distraction advice located in TS/TRV searches. MFD is the Swedish government authority responsible for guide-dog services and supplies directly applicable guidance.

## Cykel

Transportstyrelsen. Last updated 16 February 2026. [Source](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/fordonsregler/regler-for-olika-fordonsslag/cykel/).

Locator: Definitionen av en cykel, definition item 1 under Act (2001:559). Short excerpt: “Ett fordon som är avsett att drivas med tramp- eller vevanordning och inte är ett lekfordon.”.

Support: An ordinary pedal bicycle is a vehicle even though not motor-driven; a rider is operating a vehicle.

Scope: Ordinary pedal bicycle, excluding toy vehicles. No unnecessary e-bike/e-scooter taxonomy or implication that every crossing rule is identical for all vehicles.

Fallback: Exact preferred-agency definition; no additional publisher cited.

Individual mappings and source searches: evidence.json. The 70 km/h comparison follows from the §45 threshold and §43 reciprocal duties. No manufacturer or foreign sources are needed.
