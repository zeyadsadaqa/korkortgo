## Nollvisionen – tillsammans räddar vi liv

Publisher: Trafikverket. Version: Last updated/reviewed 5 November 2024. [Source](https://bransch.trafikverket.se/for-dig-i-branschen/samarbete-med-branschen/Samarbeten-for-trafiksakerhet/tillsammans-for-nollvisionen/).

Locator: Introductory aim and shared-responsibility principles 1–3. Short excerpt: “På sikt ska ingen dödas eller allvarligt skadas inom vägtransportsystemet”.

Supports: No deaths or serious injuries in the road transport system; shared responsibility of road users and system designers.

Fallback rationale: Preferred agency supplies exact support; no duplicate organisation added.

## Drowsy Driving

Publisher: NHTSA (United States government). Version: Live page; publication date not displayed in retrieved passage. [Source](https://www.nhtsa.gov/risky-driving/drowsy-driving).

Locator: Tips to Drive Alert: How To Avoid Driving Drowsy item 1; Short-Term Interventions item 1. Short excerpt: “Getting adequate sleep on a daily basis is the only true way”.

Supports: Adequate sleep is fundamental; coffee/energy drinks can give temporary perceived alertness without preventing microsleep in serious sleep deprivation.

Fallback rationale: Exact sleep-remedy and temporary-alertness distinction not located in inspected TS/TRV pages; general physiological advice applies in Sweden; US speeds and recommendations not imported.

## Microsleeps — Module 3, Negative Impacts on Sleep

Publisher: CDC/NIOSH (United States government). Version: Last reviewed 31 March 2020; Module 3, page 3 of 28. [Source](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/work-hour-training-for-nurses/longhours/mod3/03.html).

Locator: Microsleeps definition. Short excerpt: “involuntary episodes of sleep lasting a few seconds”.

Supports: Brief unintended sleep; general physiological definition, not an occupational driving rule.

Fallback rationale: Exact definition not located in inspected TS/TRV material; proposed VTI lead did not yield a retrievable exact definition.

## Drograttfylleri och sjöfylleri — proposition 1998/99:43

Publisher: Swedish Government; published by Sveriges riksdag. Version: Proposition 1998/99:43. [Source](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/proposition/drograttfylleri-och-sjofylleri_gm0343/html/).

Locator: Printed page 24, paragraph beginning De centralstimulerande medlen. Short excerpt: “kan framkalla påtagligt höjt självförtroende i förening med hyperaktivitet”.

Supports: Amphetamine/cocaine can increase confidence while concentration and clear thinking are impaired.

Fallback rationale: TRV alcohol/drug overview and TS substance reports give general impairment background but not this exact confidence claim. Historical passage used only for physiological effects, never current legal rules or obsolete warning-label advice.

## Drugs and Human Performance Fact Sheets

Publisher: NHTSA (United States government). Version: DOT HS 809 725, April 2004. [Source](https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/809725-drugshumanperformfs.pdf).

Locator: Printed pages 62–64 (PDF pages 63–65), Methamphetamine, Effects / Performance Effects / Effects on Driving. Short excerpt: “reduced fatigue or drowsiness, increased alertness”.

Supports: Perceived fatigue can diminish while risk-taking and driving impairment remain; fatigue and sleepiness may follow as drug effects change.

Fallback rationale: Exact masking claim not located in inspected TS/TRV sources. Historical pharmacology summary supports a qualified can statement about stimulant misuse, not every prescribed dose or every stimulant.
