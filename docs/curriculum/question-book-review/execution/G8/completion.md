M08-L02 defines towball load as a downward coupling force distinct from whole trailer weight. At a conventional coupling behind the rear axle, excessive force increases rear loading and unloads the front axle, impairing steering grip/response; wheels need not leave the road. An example shows why acceptable total weight does not prove acceptable coupling load. Actual equipment, axle and vehicle limits remain required; no universal numeric coupling value or hitch modification recipe is introduced.

M08-L03 explicitly teaches automatic overdue-inspection prohibition and owner responsibility without a reminder. A 31 October/1 November example distinguishes booking from completing inspection. The original deadline ban ends once inspection is carried out, but the result can establish another ban or repair/reinspection requirement. On-road restricted trips are shortest suitable routes to repair/inspection; an off-road car while still banned is restricted to the nearest inspection business, with insurance, tax and safety conditions. No automatic return-trip permission while still banned. Dangerous-defect prohibition is distinguished from an overdue ban.
