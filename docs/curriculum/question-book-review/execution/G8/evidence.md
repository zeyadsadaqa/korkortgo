## Lastbil

Transportstyrelsen. Updated 31 October 2025. [Source](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/fordonsregler/regler-for-olika-fordonsslag/personbil-lastbil-och-buss/lastbil/).

Locator: Opening definition and speed paragraph. Short excerpt: “Lätt lastbil får köra den hastighet som gäller för vägen.”.

Support: Light lorry ≤3.5 tonnes total weight; heavy >3.5; heavy 90 on motorway/expressway without trailer, 80 on other roads or towing; light without trailer follows road maximum.

Scope: Light-lorry statement explicitly scoped to no trailer. Lower road limit still binds heavy lorry; towing light lorry needs M08-L02 combination check. No inference that light lorry towing may always use road maximum.

## Towing a Trailer in Oregon — Being Equipped for Safety

Oregon Department of Transportation. Live agency-hosted 32-page brochure; publication date not printed in inspected text. [Source](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/Safety/Documents/TowingATrailer.pdf).

Locator: If You Already Have a Tow Vehicle, printed page 6 / PDF page 8; definitions/ratings pp.4–6. Short excerpt: “the tow vehicle will be less responsive to steering”.

Support: Downward tongue/hitch load defined; excessive load leaves too little weight on front wheels and reduces steering response. Follow actual vehicle/hitch ratings.

Scope: Conventional rear towball/coupling load explanation, not fifth-wheel geometry. No US percentage, hitch modification recipe, numeric load limit, registration or towing law imported. Front unloading means reduced axle load, not necessarily wheels leaving the road.

Fallback: TS/TRV kultryck searches found lifting/handling cautions and calculator scope limits, not the complete front-wheel mechanism. Exact official ODOT brochure passage selected; manufacturer fallback unnecessary.

## Besiktning av fordon

Transportstyrelsen. Updated 8 October 2025. [Source](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/aga-kopa-eller-salja-fordon/fordonsbesiktning/).

Locator: Opening owner responsibility; Vad händer om jag inte besiktar i tid? — Körförbud. Short excerpt: “Om du inte besiktar fordonet i tid, får du automatiskt körförbud”.

Support: Automatic overdue-inspection prohibition, owner responsibility even without invitation, restricted trips for on/off-road vehicle; dangerous-condition prohibition distinct.

Scope: Inträtt körförbud due missed periodic deadline, not dangerous-condition meddelat körförbud. On-road: shortest suitable route to repair workshop/inspection; off-road and still banned: shortest suitable route to nearest inspection business. Does not grant general errands/commuting or waive other requirements.

Fallback: Preferred agency support or reused current legal source.

## Vehicle Regulation (2009:211)

Swedish Government; published by Sveriges riksdag. Consolidated live text; chapter 6 §§15–21 checked 13 October 2026. [Source](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/fordonsforordning-2009211_sfs-2009-211/).

Locator: Chapter 6 §15: first calendar month after missed inspection month; §21: prohibition until inspection performed; §§16–17: defects/result. Short excerpt: “Ett körförbud enligt 15 eller 16 § gäller till dess en kontrollbesiktning har genomförts.”.

Support: Exact start/end of periodic-deadline ban; completed inspection rather than simply booking; follow new findings and any further prohibition/order.

Scope: Periodic inspection example only. Inspection being carried out ends §15 deadline ban; not a guarantee the result permits ordinary driving, since defects may create new ban/order. No implication a pass is always required merely to end the original deadline ban.

Fallback: TS page establishes ban and travel exceptions but does not state precise legal endpoint. Linked primary regulation supplies that missing detail; not an extra duplicate source for the TS-supported ban.

## Ställa av ditt fordon

Transportstyrelsen. Live page; checked 13 October 2026. [Source](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/aga-kopa-eller-salja-fordon/stall-av-eller-pa-ditt-fordon/stalla-av-ditt-fordon/).

Locator: Köra ett avställt fordon; opening: prohibition does not automatically create off-road status. Short excerpt: “Fordonet måste vara trafikförsäkrat och det får inte finnas några obetalda fordonsskatter.”.

Support: General off-road inspection exception requires traffic insurance and no unpaid vehicle taxes; ban does not automatically deregister car.

Scope: General off-road rule allows shortest suitable route to/from nearest inspection; when an overdue ban also applies, the additional ban restriction is to nearest inspection. Return is not automatically allowed while that ban continues. This is an explicit intersection of the agency conditions.

Fallback: Preferred agency support or reused current legal source.

Searches and question mappings: evidence.json. Completed inspection ends the original deadline ban; its result may establish another ban/order. Do not conflate off-road permission with permission while a ban still applies.
