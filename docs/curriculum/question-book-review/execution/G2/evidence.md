## TSFS 2017:54 – control inspection

## Taxi Driver Manual

Department for Infrastructure / Driver & Vehicle Agency, Northern Ireland government; Version 2.1 March 2024; hosted file August 2025. [Source](https://www.northernireland.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2025-08/taxi-manual-august-2025.pdf).

Locator: Wheel balance p.47; hydraulic brakes p.49; Appendix 3 Basic vehicle faults p.181 (printed/PDF numbers agree). Short excerpt: “Vibration in steering … Front wheel out of balance; brake pedal feels spongy … air in the brake system”.

Support: Wheel imbalance is a possible vibration cause; air is a possible spongy hydraulic pedal cause.

Scope: Universal vehicle principles only, not Northern Ireland taxi rules. Several faults can cause similar symptoms.

Fallback: Exact car-wheel imbalance and air/sponginess passages not located in targeted TS/TRV searches and current TS inspection text.
