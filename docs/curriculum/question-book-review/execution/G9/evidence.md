## Vägtrafikens luftföroreningar

Trafikverket. Reviewed 11 November 2021. [Source](https://bransch.trafikverket.se/for-dig-i-branschen/miljo---for-dig-i-branschen/luftkvalitet/vagtrafikens-luftfororeningar/).

Locator: Opening; Påverkan på hälsan; Partiklar; Kväveoxid; Kolmonoxid. Short excerpt: “Kolmonoxid binds hårdare till blodets hemoglobin än vad syre gör”.

Support: CO prevents oxygen transport; particles harm airways; NOx irritates mucous membranes and contributes to acidification; NOx+hydrocarbons+sunlight produce ground-level ozone; studs wear road and create breathable particles; cold emission controls less effective.

Scope: Stable qualitative mechanisms only; no historical fleet shares, diesel projections, exposure thresholds or claim outdoor CO never matters. CO and CO2 distinguished. Ground-level ozone forms via reactions, not simply directly emitted NOx.

Fallback: Preferred agency source.
