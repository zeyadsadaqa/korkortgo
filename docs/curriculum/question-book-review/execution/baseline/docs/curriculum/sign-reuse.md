## Reuse basis

Transportstyrelsen’s [Pressbilder page, Vägmärken section](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/om-oss/pressrum/pressbilder/) permits downloading and using road-sign files for different purposes without prior agency approval. It asks that their meaning be respected by using them in the proper context on the web or in print. The road-sign paragraph does not specify mandatory attribution. We will nevertheless retain an agency credit and source links in the PDF’s official-reference section.

The separate restrictions for press photographs on that page must not be substituted for, or overridden by, the road-sign paragraph. This record does not claim a Creative Commons licence, public-domain status, permission for unrelated photographs or branding, or an official endorsement of the learning material.

**Project credit:** “Road-sign illustrations: Transportstyrelsen. Source links and verification dates are recorded in the official references.” This is a project traceability choice, not a quotation of a required credit line. Preserve the manuscript’s learning-aid disclaimer.

**Catalogue scope interpretation:** The official catalogue also supplies markings, traffic signals, police/guard directions and railway signals as downloadable illustrations. Applying its road-sign-file permission to those catalogue files is our documented interpretation of the linked collection. It is not a separate explicit licence for each family. Use only the linked catalogue artwork in its teaching context; do not extend that interpretation to outside photographs, equipment art or independently drawn scenes.
