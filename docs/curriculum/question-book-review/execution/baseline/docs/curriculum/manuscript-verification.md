## Source and writing decisions

The approved outline version 1.0 remains the teaching sequence. The manuscript was written independently around those objectives, using official evidence rather than adapting a publisher's chapter text, examples or questions. Learner-facing text and new evidence files contain no background-book names or original book-page references.

Trafikverket and Transportstyrelsen are the primary guidance sources. The detailed Traffic Regulation and Road Signs Regulation, as published by Sveriges riksdag, provide official primary support for exceptions that the agency overview pages do not explain. A VTI research report commissioned by and hosted on Transportstyrelsen supports limited friction/ABS principles; it is labelled research, not legislation or a universal vehicle-operation manual.

The user authorised a brief reference to 1177 for first aid during step 3. M10-L02 retains scene safety, emergency contact and accident duties, with a short first-aid signpost. It does not reproduce clinical procedures, pulse checks or a medical course. The syllabus includes incident response and first-aid learning; no claim is made that the subject is excluded from official assessment.

Legal requirements, official recommendations and original teaching exercises are distinguished in the manuscript. Reference dates identify when evidence was checked, not a guarantee that it remains unchanged. The Traffic Regulation consolidation includes future text effective 1 December 2026; the trailer-speed discussion uses the provision applicable on 1 October 2026, not the future version.

## Review performed

- Checked the actual manuscript against the approved module and lesson titles and all 50 lesson/reflection IDs.
- Checked every lesson for an explanation, worked decision, knowledge check with answer, and reminder; every module has an applied review and self-assessment.
- Checked reference definitions, used aliases and source IDs against the saved source manifest; every cited source has a recorded successful retrieval and date.
- Reviewed the legally significant numbers and exceptions against the provisions or guidance identified in the evidence register.
- Checked the original arithmetic: speed conversion at 50/80 km/h, the one-second response assumption, 2,200 + 1,500 kg entitlement example and 400 + 500 kg actual trailer example.
- Checked the prominent introduction disclaimer, brief 1177 scope, official-only source domains and absence of background-book names/page references.
- Reviewed potentially misleading absolutes: green signals, stop/parking distinctions, priority, child height, tyre compliance, assistance systems, motorway eligibility, emergency response and examination readiness.

These are document/content checks, not application tests. No app code, questions, sign assets or existing source records were migrated. No rendered-layout verification is claimed for this Markdown manuscript.

## Boundaries and remaining work

There is no unmarked factual placeholder in a lesson. Model-specific controls, maintenance, recovery and energy procedures are deliberately not generalised. Unusual vehicle combinations, special legal exemptions and an individual's medical/entitlement decisions still require the relevant current official information and vehicle instructions; this book is not an exhaustive legal manual.

1. **Step 4:** Prepare the official illustrated sign reference and lesson placements. The textual sign-study appendix does not complete graphic coverage, variants, labelling, reuse records or print quality.

4. **Steps 7–10:** Perform the separately requested migration, app design, implementation and verification work.
