## Electronic Stability Control Systems — final-rule technical appendix

US Department of Transportation, NHTSA. Docket NHTSA–2007–27662; 2007 prepublication final-rule PDF, 327 pages. [Source](https://www.nhtsa.gov/document/final-rule-federal-motor-vehicle-safety-standards-electronic-stability-control-systems-0).

Locator: What NHTSA Means by Mitigating Excessive Understeer, Case 1, PDF/numbered p.310; Case 3 p.311. Short excerpt: “how sharply the vehicle can turn is limited by the friction”.

Fallback: VTI report gives understeer path and axle-grip framework but not the explicit front-axle limit passage in plain detail. NHTSA appendix supplies exact front-contact mechanism.
