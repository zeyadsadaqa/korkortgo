## Traffic Regulation (1998:1276)

Swedish Government; published by Sveriges riksdag. Consolidated live text; relevant current provisions checked 13 October 2026. [Source](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/trafikforordning-19981276_sfs-1998-1276/).

Locator: Chapter 3 §§24, 26, 29 and 46; chapter 9 §§1–2. Short excerpt: “En förare får vända eller backa endast om det kan ske utan fara eller hinder för andra vägtrafikanter.”.

Support: Turning/leaving road must protect oncoming and same-direction/rear traffic; turning around must avoid danger/obstruction; motorway/expressway turnaround and reversing prohibited.

Scope: These provisions constrain the conditional editorial example; they do not explicitly recommend return-right routing. Other signs, access restrictions, controls and local rules remain applicable.

Fallback: Trafikverket example does not specify the complete general legal boundary. Current Swedish statute supplies precise constraints rather than inventing an unconditional route recommendation.
