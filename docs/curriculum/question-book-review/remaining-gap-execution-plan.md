## H4 execution record

**Approved and completed 13 October 2026.** Added two M03-L04 teaching pages for the explicitly approximate exercise model:

- Define v in km/h and t in seconds.
- Rough reaction: R = (v / 10) × t × 3 metres.
- Rough braking: B = (v / 10)² × 0.4 metres.
- Rough stopping total: S = R + B.

Retained exact constant-speed reaction conversion, (v / 3.6) × t, and the separate squared-speed/braking comparison. Explained that rough reaction is exactly 8% above conversion; the braking coefficient assumes good brakes/tyres and dry asphalt and is not a real-world stopping prediction. Trafiko is commercial shortcut provenance only; variable-time multiplication is editorial arithmetic. Trafikverket supports principles and condition dependence, not the shortcut constants. The retained VGU reference keeps its original retrieval limitation.

Worked examples cover 40 km/h/0.5 s = 12.4 m, 70 km/h/1 s = 40.6 m and 50 km/h/2 s = 40 m. Exact reaction in the last example is about 27.8 m and is kept separate. Practice answers remain non-facing.

## H5 execution record

**Approved and completed 13 October 2026.** Added two M09-L02 pages teaching:

- Journey litres = distance in km × consumption in litres/100 km / 100.
- Litres saved = distance in km × supplied reduction in litres/100 km / 100.
- Subtract consumption rates first when two are given; retain units/decimals and round only the final result under a stated convention.

Examples teach 120 km at 6.5 litres/100 km = 7.8 litres, a 1.5 reduction over 300 km = 4.5 litres saved, and a two-rate comparison. Equations and rounding are editorial arithmetic; Transportstyrelsen supplies economical-driving context only. Supplied savings are exercise assumptions, not guaranteed technique benefits.
