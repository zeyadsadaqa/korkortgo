## Board 1

Large beautiful typography, simple line icons, subtle abstract Swedish countryside road illustration on home only. No gradients or flashy decoration. Same navigation in each: simple road-symbol logo “Körkort”, “Overview”, “Theory library”, “Practice”. Small “English · Category B” badge. Label screens outside each frame.
Screen 01 Overview: headline “Your road to confidence.” subtitle “Learn the rules. Understand the why. Practise at your pace.” primary “Start a practice test”, secondary “Explore the theory”. Small stats “1,000+ practice questions”, “5 knowledge areas”, “2026 edition”. Five category cards with EXACT category labels: “Vehicle knowledge & maneuvering”, “Environment”, “Traffic safety”, “Traffic rules”, “Personal conditions”. Editorial rather than dashboard clutter.
Screen 02 Theory library: headline “Understand every journey.” Search field “Search topics, rules and road signs”. Left category filter; main topic cards “Priority & junctions”, “Stopping & parking”, “Pedestrians & cyclists”, “Road signs”, “Driving in winter”, “Motorways”. Each card short descriptor and “Read topic →”. Supporting sources section “Theory Book 2026” and “Transportstyrelsen”.
Screen 03 Book reader: breadcrumb “Theory library / Traffic rules”; title “Priority & junctions”. Two-column layout chapter list left, readable illustrated article right. Use original explanatory summary UI not fabricated PDF scan. Clear subsection “Give way to the right”, a simple top-down two-car junction illustration, callout “Road width does not determine priority.” Source chip “Theory Book · pages 22–44”. Buttons “Previous topic” and “Next topic”, reading progress.
Screen 04 Test setup: headline “Make this practice yours.” Two-step form. “1. How many questions?” editable number 70, slider 1 to 70, presets 10 / 20 / 40 / 70. “2. When should answers be revealed?” two selectable cards “After each question” / “At the end of the test”, first selected. Beside form show allocation list EXACTLY Vehicle knowledge & maneuvering 7; Environment 5; Traffic safety 16; Traffic rules 32; Personal conditions 5; Random from any category 5. Footer “70 questions · No time pressure”; green “Start test →”. No sign-in wall, no paid features, no invented progress. Render all four screens fully and cleanly.

## Board 2

Use case: ui-mockup. Create BOARD 2: PRACTISE & REVIEW for the “Körkort” Swedish driving theory app. High-resolution four complete desktop website screens in a 2x2 grid, 3840x2160 presentation board, flat UI, no perspective devices. Consistent Scandinavian editorial UI: warm ivory #F7F8F3, forest green #173E32, muted sage panels, charcoal text, mustard accents, thin borders, tasteful rounded cards; serif editorial headings and readable sans body. Navigation identical in each screen: “Körkort”, “Overview”, “Theory library”, “Practice”, “English · Category B”. Label each screen outside the frame. Do not add login, payments or timers.
Screen 05 Question: top “Practice test” and “Question 12 of 70”; thin progress bar, category badge “Traffic rules”. Large question “A police officer signals you to stop, but the traffic light is green. What should you do?” A minimal illustrated officer holding a stop hand beside a green light, calm small educational illustration. Four large stacked radio choices: A “Follow the green light”; B “Stop as directed by the officer”; C “Stop only if there is a stop sign”; D “Continue if the road looks clear”. B is selected but not marked correct yet. Bottom “Answers revealed at the end” and primary “Confirm answer →”. Right small pane “Your test” with neutral numbered progress circles showing answered versus remaining, never correctness in at-end mode. Plenty of whitespace.
Screen 06 Immediate feedback: same question after a WRONG selection A. Header “Let’s understand this one.” Four visible choices A red tinted “Your answer”, B green tinted “Correct answer”, C/D neutral. Below an explanation panel with four concise item-specific explanations: B “Police directions take precedence over signals, signs and general rules.” A “A green light does not override the officer.” C “The officer’s instruction is enough; no stop sign is needed.” D “An empty road does not cancel the instruction.” Source link “Theory Book 2026 · page 8”. CTA “Next question →”. Clear error text, not colour alone.
Screen 07 Results: headline “Every question is a step forward.” Large circular score “56 / 70” and “80% correct”; small cards “56 correct”, “14 to revisit”. Five category bars: Vehicle knowledge & maneuvering 6/8, Environment 4/5, Traffic safety 14/17, Traffic rules 27/34, Personal conditions 5/6. Counts total 70, these include the five extra random questions. Primary “Review answers”; secondary “Take another test”. Footnote “Practice score · not an official exam result.” No pass/fail claim.
Screen 08 Answer review: title “Turn mistakes into understanding.” Filter pills “All 70”, “Incorrect 14”, “Correct 56”. Accordion list of questions with explicit correct/incorrect labels. Expand first item the same police question with wrong selected A and correct B plus the SAME four specific option explanations. Source “Theory Book 2026 · page 8” and “Read topic →”. Compact second and third collapsed items about parking and vehicle tyres. Bottom “Take another test →”. Make all major text crisp, accurate and readable.

Bottom nav Home / Learn / Practice only on home/library/reader; test screens use minimal focused navigation and Quit text.
