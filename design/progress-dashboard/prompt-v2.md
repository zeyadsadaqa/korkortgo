## Generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a high fidelity design review board with THREE large mobile screens side by side for KörkortGo, an English-language Swedish driving theory app. Landscape board, high resolution, each phone screen same approximately 390x844 logical-pixel ratio. Straight-on flat UI, no perspective, no hand, no bulky device frames. Quiet neutral background, narrow gaps. Above the screens small labels "01 / HOME", "02 / LEARNING", "03 / EXERCISE"; small board footer "Design proposal · Illustrative progress data". Exact bottom-navigation labels on ALL three screens: "Home", "Learning", "Exercise", icons plus words, fixed at bottom with safe area, selected item sage rounded pill. Home selected first screen, Learning second, Exercise third.
Brand: forest #173E32, paper #F7F8F3, sage #E7EEE5, ink #172C29, white cards with fine #D5DED5 borders, dark secondary text on sage. Source Serif 4-style headings, Source Sans 3-style readable body. Refined Scandinavian mobile product, compact typography, 16px body, 20px margins, 16px corners, 48-52px buttons, subtle outlined icons. No giant marketing hero, no huge decorative art, no gradients, no shadows. Content must fit realistic mobile screen with vertical scrolling implied, no overlap with nav.
